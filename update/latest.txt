v2.1

RELEASE NOTES:

Add support for making iOS 17.5 ramdisks for A12/A13 devices in surrealSSHRD option and add support for creating FakeVar on A12/A13 running iOS 18 and 26 (developers only).
Bring back iOS 8 seprmvr64 support (8.0-8.2 only), fix XS Max restores on Linux, add jailbreak support for iOS 8 tether downgrades (macOS only).

Also fix baseband on A8 iOS 8/9 seprmvr64 tether downgrades (iPad Air 2 is not included). Implement untethered downgrades to iOS 8.0-8.2 with blobs for iPhone 5S, although entirely experimental (functionality is also broken) and not fully tested yet.
Add back macOS 10.15 - 11.x support. And support MacPorts as additional package manager on macOS.
